   -> Operating Tower
   -> 3 Titan + 2 Bastion

   goal: Set up your economy and get ready for Cyclops production
2. when Lore of Flux: 2 Prospector
   -> "Assembly Plants" (loop Cyclops)
   -> "Barracks" (loop Bastion)
   goal: Scale up Cyclops and Bastion production
3. when a lot of hexite: Operating Tower + 2 Barracks
   -> Operating Tower + 2 Barracks^1
   goal: Grow your economy and your production
4. when third base: 2 Prospector + Mechanical Research Lab + Research Lab
   -> "Barracks" (loop Commando)
   -> "Assembly Plants" (loop Cyclops)

^1: Repeat whenever a lot of hexite piles up.

## Production and economy

The first [Cyclops](/wiki/protectorate/unit/cyclops) may come out late if you cannot afford it right away. That is fine, because the two extra [Prospectors](/wiki/protectorate/unit/prospector) from [Lore of Flux](/wiki/arandi/talent/lore-of-flux) (talent level 2) give you the economy to keep it going.

Keep expanding whenever a lot of hexite piles up. Once you have six Prospectors, add a third [Assembly Plant](/wiki/protectorate/building/assembly-plant) to produce more Cyclops.

When there are no bases left to take, spend the remaining hexite on extra [Barracks](/wiki/protectorate/building/barracks) and keep producing [Commandos](/wiki/protectorate/unit/commando).

## Against mass Skrells

If your opponent goes for mass [Skrells](/wiki/grell/unit/skrell), do not switch to Cyclops and keep producing [Titans](/wiki/protectorate/unit/titan) from your Assembly Plants instead. Titans need less flux than Cyclops, so you can afford more Assembly Plants than in the normal build.

## Juggernaut support

Build a [War Foundry](/wiki/protectorate/building/war-foundry) as you get close to the [Force Multiplier](/wiki/protectorate/talent/force-multiplier) talent. Do not produce anything from it, since it is only there so that [Reinforcements](/wiki/protectorate/talent/reinforcements) can call down [Juggernauts](/wiki/protectorate/unit/juggernaut) instead of Commandos.

## Army composition

Cyclops are your main source of damage and come out of your Assembly Plants all game long. Commandos replace the [Bastions](/wiki/protectorate/unit/bastion) once you take your third base, and their range upgrade [Magnetic Rifiling](/wiki/protectorate/upgrade/magnetic-rifiling) comes first. Juggernauts support the front line and are especially good against swarming armies.

Titans are your early game investment and matter most when your opponent goes for mass Skrells, while Bastions only carry the early game until Commandos take over.

## Early game micro

This build relies on keeping your early Titans alive for as long as possible, with [Rapid Reconstitution](/wiki/protectorate/talent/rapid-reconstitution) making [Beam Up](/wiki/protectorate/topbar/beam-up) faster. Spend all of your topbar power on beaming up Titans and Bastions at half health, and use the shield of [Marv](/wiki/protectorate/hero/marv) when needed to keep them alive while they beam up.
