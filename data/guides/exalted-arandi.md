## Key timings

- Take the mid XP tower first with Galavax, then a side tower. The enemy hero usually goes for the mid tower while you take the side one, or right after.
- That is exactly when your first two Exalted come out, so you know where the enemy hero is and you can snipe it with the Galavax Spear and the reanimated Exalted.
- Spend all your energy on Reanimate for the Exalted. As soon as you have some, every Exalted should be reanimated.
- Kraegar is a big power spike, which is why you take B2 at that moment, since you are very hard to dislodge. Harass without overcommitting because you cannot take a base yet, hunt harvesters across the map and lock the opponent in their base.
- In phase 3, hero upgrades win the game, so take all of them without hesitation.

## Economy

- Lore of Flux (talent level 2) brings you to 6 harvesters, which is all the flux the hero upgrades need, so do not build more.

## Against Skrells and air

- Mix in Thralls from B2 on. Exalted alone cannot fight Skrells, especially when big units like Valkaru Hounds back them up.
- Build close to the flux nodes so your harvesters are hard to target when Skrells harass, especially the Bestiaries.
- Upgrade every base to a Garrison Tower Turret so it attacks.
- Do not hesitate to add an extra tower next to the flux nodes where your harvesters work, on top of the base, to protect them.

## Against Protectorate Marran

- As soon as you scout Badgers, stay on B1 and fast tech Kraegar's Explosive Warhead. Played well, it lands around 6:30.
- Do not hesitate to start with Kraegar and get Inquisitress second. She is stronger than Galavax against Badgers, and Kraegar first lets you fast tech the nuke safely.
- Do not take B2 and watch your flux. If you cannot hide your harvesters, take Efficient Upgrades instead of Lore of Flux at talent level 2.
