```bo
1. Idal Conduit + 2 Bestiary (loop Exalted) (-> 3 Legion Labourer) + Citadel
   -> Idal Conduit + Altar
   -> Kraegar + Hallowed Strike
   goal: Take map control and the 3 XP towers
2. when Kraegar starts: Garrison Tower + Idal Conduit + Terror Tower + Armory + Arandi Penumbra
   -> More Feed + Explosive Warhead
   goal: Kraegar out, take B2 and fast tech
3. Inquisitress + Arandi Judgement + Lightning Spear + Improved Charge + Wake of Fire + Burning Fireball
   -> Garrison Tower + Idal Conduit + 2 Bestiary^1
   goal: Late game, every hero upgrade, close the game
4. Dreadnought [unit]
   goal: Dreadnought finisher
^1: 2 Bestiary per Garrison Tower.
```

## Key timings

- Take the mid XP tower first with [Galavax](/wiki/the-legion/hero/galavax), then a side tower. The enemy hero usually goes for the mid tower while you take the side one, or right after.
- That is exactly when your first two [Exalted](/wiki/the-legion/unit/exalted) come out, so you know where the enemy hero is and you can snipe it with the Galavax Spear and the reanimated Exalted.
- Spend all your energy on [Reanimate](/wiki/the-legion/topbar/reanimate) for the Exalted. As soon as you have some, every Exalted should be reanimated.
- [Kraegar](/wiki/the-legion/hero/kraegar) is a big power spike, which is why you take B2 at that moment, since you are very hard to dislodge. Harass without overcommitting because you cannot take a base yet, hunt harvesters across the map and lock the opponent in their base.
- In phase 3, hero upgrades win the game, so take all of them without hesitation.

## Economy

- [Lore of Flux](/wiki/arandi/talent/lore-of-flux) (talent level 2) brings you to 6 harvesters, which is all the flux the hero upgrades need, so do not build more.

## Against Skrells and air

Skip the [Terror Tower](/wiki/the-legion/building/terror-tower) and the [Armory](/wiki/the-legion/building/armory) in phase 2: go straight for 2 [Barracks](/wiki/the-legion/building/barracks) to mass [Thralls](/wiki/the-legion/unit/thrall), and get the [Arandi Judgement](/wiki/arandi/unit/arandi-judgement) first.

```bo
2. when Kraegar starts: Garrison Tower + Idal Conduit + 2 Barracks (loop Thrall) + Arandi Penumbra
   -> Arandi Judgement
   goal: Kraegar out, take B2 and hold the air
```

- Mix in Thralls from B2 on. Exalted alone cannot fight [Skrells](/wiki/grell/unit/skrell), especially when big units like [Valkaru Hounds](/wiki/valkaru/unit/valkaru-hound) back them up.
- Build close to the flux nodes so your harvesters are hard to target when Skrells harass, especially the [Bestiaries](/wiki/the-legion/building/bestiary).
- Upgrade every base to a [Garrison Tower Turret](/wiki/the-legion/building/garrison-tower-turret) so it attacks.
- Do not hesitate to add an extra tower next to the flux nodes where your harvesters work, on top of the base, to protect them.

## Against Marran Badgers

Play this phase 2 only when you scout [Marran Badgers](/wiki/marran/unit/marran-badger), not against every Protectorate Marran. Stay on B1 and fast tech Kraegar's [Explosive Warhead](/wiki/the-legion/upgrade/explosive-warhead).

```bo
2. when Altar done: Inquisitress + Armory + Terror Tower
   -> More Feed + Explosive Warhead
   goal: Explosive Warhead around 6:30, no B2
```

- Do not hesitate to start with Kraegar and get [Inquisitress](/wiki/the-legion/hero/inquisitress) second. She is stronger than Galavax against Badgers, and Kraegar first lets you fast tech the nuke safely.
- Do not take B2 and watch your flux. If you cannot hide your harvesters, take [Efficient Upgrades](/wiki/the-legion/talent/efficient-upgrades) instead of Lore of Flux at talent level 2.
