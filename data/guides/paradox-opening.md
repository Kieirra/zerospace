## Key timings

- **Early game:** Focus on protecting your Flux harvesters with [I'olin](/wiki/arandi/hero/iolin) and [Arandi Phoenix Guards](/wiki/arandi/unit/arandi-phoenix-guard). Avoid spending Flux on anything that delays your Tier 4 tech. Keep your key Flux units alive, as you will need them to merge into [Paradox](/wiki/the-xol/unit/paradox) later. Giving up early map control is part of the strategy, so do not take unnecessary fights.

- **Paradox timing:** The moment you merge your first Paradox, immediately switch to offense. This is your biggest power spike and your opponent's window to react is limited. Your priority is to deal significant damage, ideally destroying their third base while reclaiming XP towers with your [Rollers](/wiki/the-xol/unit/roller). If the engagement becomes dangerous, use [Recall](/wiki/the-xol/topbar/recall) to save your Paradox. Losing it can completely ruin your momentum.

- **Replicate priority:** Prioritize using [Replicate](/wiki/the-xol/topbar/replicate) on expensive Flux units, especially [Synapses](/wiki/the-xol/unit/synapse), until you have completed all your [World Forge](/wiki/the-xol/building/world-forge) upgrades. This allows you to build up a powerful army while maintaining your upgrade progression.

- **Upgrade choices:** Your choice between [Range](/wiki/the-xol/upgrade/weapon-range) and [Armor](/wiki/the-xol/upgrade/armor) should depend on your opponent's army composition. Against large numbers of Tier 1 units, Armor is generally the better option. Otherwise, Range can provide more value.

- **Late game:** Your late-game composition revolves around a highly advanced army, primarily consisting of Tier 3 units, including [Echo](/wiki/the-xol/unit/echo). Transition into [Eradicators](/wiki/the-xol/unit/eradicator), [Aegis](/wiki/the-xol/unit/aegis), Echoes and Paradoxes to maintain your advantage as the game progresses.

## Economy

- [Lore of Flux](/wiki/arandi/talent/lore-of-flux) (talent level 2) allows you to reach your Harvester limit much earlier, accelerating your economy and helping you afford your World Forge upgrades sooner.

## Against one-base all-ins

If you scout a one-base opening, prioritize survival over your Tier 4 timing.

- Upgrade your natural [World Engine](/wiki/the-xol/building/world-engine) into a [World Engine Turret](/wiki/the-xol/building/world-engine-turret) to strengthen your defenses.
- Produce all available units necessary to survive the incoming attack, even if it delays your tech progression.
- Protect your Flux harvesters and avoid losing important units unnecessarily.
- Once the threat has been dealt with and your economy is secure, resume your original Tier 4 tech path.

Your primary objective is to survive the aggression without taking crippling economic damage. Once stabilized, your superior tech progression will allow you to regain control of the game.
